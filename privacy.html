Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Toolz handles information and third-party advertising.

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Toolz.run.place ("Toolz", "we", "our", or "us"). Toolz is a free online tools website operated by Prabhasha Laknindu.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website and online tools. We aim to provide useful, fast and privacy-focused tools while keeping unnecessary data collection to a minimum.

2. Information We Collect

Most Toolz tools are designed to work directly in your web browser. We do not intentionally collect or store personal information submitted to our browser-based tools.

Our current frontend tools process information locally in your browser whenever the tool is designed for browser-based processing. We do not operate a database that stores the files or text processed through these tools.

However, normal technical information may be processed automatically by web hosting, browsers, advertising services or other third-party services required to deliver website content.

3. Browser-Based Tools

Several Toolz services are designed to operate directly on your device. For example, browser-based tools such as image processing, QR generation and text processing can perform their functions without requiring the user's files to be uploaded to our server.

Because processing behavior can differ between tools and future versions, users should review the information displayed on the relevant tool page before submitting sensitive information.

4. Advertising

Toolz may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers, including Adsterra. We may use advertising formats such as banner advertisements and social bar advertisements.

In the future, Toolz may also use Google AdSense or other advertising services. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, device information, IP addresses or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Toolz does not control the information collected directly by third-party advertising providers. Users should review the privacy policies of those providers to understand how their information may be handled.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Toolz does not intentionally set its own advertising or tracking cookies for user profiling. However, third-party services, including advertising providers, may use cookies or similar technologies when their content is displayed on our website.

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of some websites or third-party services.

6. Google AdSense

Toolz may use Google AdSense in the future to display advertisements. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements according to Google's applicable policies and user settings.

For information about Google's advertising practices and available controls, users should refer to Google's official privacy and advertising information.

7. Third-Party Links and Services

Toolz may contain links to third-party websites, services or resources. These websites operate independently from Toolz and have their own privacy policies and terms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security practices of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party service you visit.

8. Children's Privacy

Toolz is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at collecting personal information from children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through our browser-based tools.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain the security and availability of our website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, advertising providers, technology or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
