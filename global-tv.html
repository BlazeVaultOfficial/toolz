About Global TV

Global TV is a channel directory. It reads channel metadata and stream links from a public third-party playlist; it does not host, record or redistribute video files. Channel names, logos and stream availability may change without notice.

A public listing is not a guarantee that a stream is licensed for every viewer or territory. Please access a channel only where you have the right to watch it. Respect broadcaster terms, copyright rules and local laws.

Playback & privacy: When you select a channel, your browser connects directly to the selected stream provider. That provider may receive your IP address and connection details. Some streams may be offline, region-restricted, DRM-protected, blocked by CORS, or unsupported by your browser. This page cannot repair problems on a broadcaster's server.
