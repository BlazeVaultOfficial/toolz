Country Analytics & Travel Planner

Toolz Country Analytics is a free interactive country information tool designed for students, travelers, researchers and anyone who wants to quickly explore information about countries around the world.

🌍 Explore Country Information

Search for a country and instantly view its population, capital city, region, subregion, official languages, currency, time zones and geographic coordinates.

💱 Currency Exchange Rates

The currency section allows you to select currencies and calculate conversions using exchange-rate data. Rates are provided through ExchangeRate-API's Open Access endpoint.

🗺️ Interactive Maps

The interactive map displays the selected country's location and geographic boundary. You can zoom, move around the map and inspect neighboring countries.

🧭 Bordering Countries

When country data includes land borders, each neighboring country appears as a clickable button. Selecting a border country loads its information without requiring a new search.

✈️ Useful for Travel Planning

Country information can be useful when preparing for international travel, comparing destinations, checking currencies, understanding languages and exploring geographical relationships between countries.

Country data is provided through countries.dev. Currency-rate data is provided through ExchangeRate-API Open Access. Data availability, update frequency and service limits are determined by the respective providers.
